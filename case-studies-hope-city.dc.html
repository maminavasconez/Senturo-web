How Hope City Houston Secures Its Devices with Senturo

Hope City Houston, a multi-campus church with a rapidly growing congregation, faced increasing challenges managing their expanding technology infrastructure. With 150 devices across multiple locations, they turned to Senturo for a comprehensive device management solution.

image / Hope City Houston logo
About the client

About Hope City Houston

Hope City Houston is more than a church—it’s a thriving community committed to “Loving God, loving people, and changing the world.” With multiple campuses, an active digital presence, and a growing congregation, the church relies on technology to power its mission. From managing worship services to running outreach programs, their devices play a crucial role in keeping everything running smoothly.

01 · The challenge

The Challenge: Keeping Tabs on 150 Devices

Joshua Rhodes, IT Coordinator at Hope City Houston, oversees all church technology. As the congregation expanded, so did their collection of iPads, MacBooks, and other mobile devices used for services, administration, and events. Without an efficient tracking system, managing and securing these devices across multiple campuses became increasingly difficult, prompting the search for a more advanced solution.

Key concerns:

  • Device security: Preventing loss and theft.
  • Efficient management: Reducing time spent manually tracking devices.
  • Real-time monitoring: Knowing where every device is at all times.“We needed a solution to keep tabs on our devices,”

Joshua explains. “And a solution that would simplify our workload, not add to it”

02 · The solution

The Solution: Senturo = A Game Changer

After evaluating different options, Hope City Houston chose Senturo for its ease of deployment, user-friendly dashboard, and powerful security features. One of the key advantages was its seamless integration with Jamf Pro, the church’s existing Mobile Device Management (MDM) system. This allowed them to streamline device tracking while maintaining their established workflows.

Why Senturo?

  • Simple setup - quick and seamless implementation.
  • Easy-to-use online dashboard - track every device in one place.
  • Powerful security tools - mitigate theft and protect church assets.
  • Works alongside Jamf Pro - enhancing their existing MDM system for better oversight.
03 · The results

The Results: Measurable Impact & Peace of Mind

Since deploying Senturo, Hope City Houston has seen real, tangible benefits:

  • Lost Device? Recovered! - “It’s already helped us recover a stolen laptop.”
  • Time Savings -“Automating device tracking means fewer work hours spent on it.”
  • Reliable Support - “Senturo’s non-profit pricing and extended 3-year renewal made it an even better fit for us.”
04 · Final verdict

Final Verdict: “Senturo Just Works.”

With Senturo in place, Hope City Houston can focus on building its community - not worrying about lost or stolen tech. Now, their devices stay as connected, protected, and mission-ready as the people who use them.
