How Catoosa Public Schools Ensures Student Safety Through Faster Device Recovery

At Catoosa Public Schools, Samuel Hoch, Director of Technology, and his team ensure the students’ ability to learn effectively. With a vast network and numerous IT assets to oversee, the school district sought a robust solution to track down lost devices to limit the amount of downtime for students.

image / Catoosa logo or campus
About the client

About Catoosa Public Schools

Catoosa Public Schools, located in Catoosa, Oklahoma, educates approximately 1,800 students across four schools, spread over nine sites, from pre-kindergarten through 12th grade. The district is committed to providing a high-quality education, focusing on academic excellence and holistic development.

01 · The challenge

The Challenge: Managing 2,000 Chromebooks across four schools and 9 sites

One of Catoosa’s learning principles is ensuring each student has the learning technology to support a superior education. Each student receives a school-owned Chromebook.

But, with almost 2,000 Chromebooks at large across four schools and 9 sites, it wasn’t always easy for Samuel Hoch, Director of Technology, to stay in control. “I’m responsible for all technology in the school district. From Chromebooks to iPads, to printers and phones, TVs, switches, networking components, and internet circuits - the works,” says Samuel. “Attempting” to find lost devices without an effective way of doing so costs in hours of time and money.“

Tired of the lose-replace cycle

Samuel was tired of the lose-replace cycle, but that wasn't the only problem. “We’re big on a sense of responsibility so, before we replace a device, we always ask the student to search for the device for a few days. It’s not an immediate replacement, nor should it be. But it does mean that the student is without a device for a few days and this means they lose out on their education. We needed to solve both of these problems fast.”

02 · The solution

The Solution: (Almost) From Day One, Senturo Tracked Down Devices

When Samuel and his team started researching tools to help them easily track lost devices, he was worried that the setup would be difficult. He was positively surprised when "that wasn't the case at all. It was just so easy to install, and we had it up and running in minutes."

With Senturo installed, Samuel could track down lost devices in seconds. “It proved to be invaluable within days of setting it up,’ he says. “We've used it on numerous occasions to locate lost or stolen Chromebooks, and saved thousands of dollars compared to previous years of not having tracking software.

It’s a win for the student, too, as speedy locating means they’re not without their device for long. And if the device is stolen rather than just lost, we know about it immediately. We’re never left wondering. There’s no ambiguity.”

03 · Beyond recovery

More than just recovering Chromebooks: Locating a lost student

Samuel’s team discovered additional benefits of working with Senturo during the implementation. The District is serious about education, which means it’s serious about attendance. It expects 90% attendance from each student, and a legitimate reason for non-attendance every time. Senturo has given the District a mechanism to support attendance.

"Students aren’t tracked,” says Samuel, “but if students have reported into school but aren’t actually there, we want to know why. We've used Senturo more than once to locate students who have done this, and that alone is worth the price. The earlier we can fix attendance issues, the better the outcomes for the student.”

"We have had specific situations where students skipped school, and not even their parents knew where they were. It is invaluable for us to be able to locate these students."

04 · The result

The Result: Saving the IT team time

Senturo has supported Samuel and his team to make their roles easier. “I truly enjoy helping students get the best education possible, and, in my role, that means keeping them connected, assisting them and solving their problems fast, ”says Samuel. “When I’m contacted to track a device, I can now answer, "Yes - I'll get right on that." Before Senturo, I had to explain that we didn't have a way to track.”

Why Catoosa Public Schools selected Senturo

  • Instant tracking to locate lost devices rather than spend money on replacements:

    The time without a device was reduced to the minimum in the case of lost devices, ensuring that the school could keep delivering a good education for all students.

  • An early indication of students skipping school for proactive communication:

    An easy way to track students’ attendance to ensure timely communication in the case of challenges on the students’ side.

  • Reasonably priced, making for a good balance between time saved and money spent.
