Relax: Senturo gives you the power to geo-track and locate your mobile devices but also to set geofences to get alerted on device movements and automatically lock them down if they wander too far. All from a single, user-friendly multi-OS dashboard.

Automate IT Policies

Set Up Geofencing & Alerts

Keep your computers where they should be. Simply set the territory (from meters to thousands of miles) and you’ll get alerts when a device leaves its designated zone, and also when it returns. This tightens security and is also useful for managing mobile and remote healthcare workers with ease, too.

Block Unauthorized Networks

Safeguard data by making sure that your in-home or mobile healthcare workers and clinicians stay firmly on authorized networks. If a device tries to connect to unauthorized VPNs, personal hotspots, or insecure networks, you’ll be notified immediately.

One-Way Broadcasts to All Devices

Make organizing people and processes easier with one-way messaging to one, some or all of your devices - your choice. Customize your messages and schedule them in advance, to automate delivery for whenever you like. Send critical alerts for clinic closures, fire drill reminders, or natural hazard instructions. Send gentle reminders for device returns, too.
