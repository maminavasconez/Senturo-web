Absolute, handled openly

Don't attack it and don't assume anything. Find out: coverage, what they value, gaps, effort, contract timing, and whether they need a complement now or an alternative later.

“Do more with a lean device-management team.”

Ask

  1. With limited device staff, where is the most manual effort going?
  2. Can you see one consistent picture of the whole device fleet?
  3. For a random student device, how fast can you tell who last used it?
  4. How does Absolute fit into that today, and where does it stop?
  5. Would device and network data in your existing dashboards help?

Opener

“Lenny suggested we connect, and he speaks very highly of you.” Keep it brief, then move to his priorities.

Avoid

Leading with lost or stolen devices. Attacking Absolute. Pitching an MDM, ITSM or EDR. Presenting the API as plumbing.

Limited staff → Fleet scope → Asset/lifecycle → User accountability → Automation/delegation → API → Absolute → Recovery
