At a glance

How to run each meeting

Diagnose first. Position second. Demo only what matters.

Use the first part of the meeting to understand the problem, fleet and workflow. If there is a clear fit, ask permission to show 1–2 relevant product moments for 60–180 seconds. The goal is an agreed next step.
