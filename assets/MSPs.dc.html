Use cases

Be ready for whatever your clients need

Senturo gives your team the visibility and recovery tools to act fast, resolve problems quickly and give your clients an even stronger service they can count on.

See all your clients in one clear view

Monitor devices, locations, and activity for all your client fleets from a single dashboard. Easily organize devices with tags and groups so you can find what you need anytime.

Find missing devices faster

Device gone missing? Track it down fast: review its latest location, location history, recent activity, and its last logged-in user. Give your team the information they need to deploy the right recovery action.

Get alerts when a device leaves an approved area

Create geofences around offices, schools and other approved locations, then configure alerts and automated actions to help your team respond when a device moves beyond its designated boundary.

Build a complete recovery record

Export detailed reports of devices, users, dates, and locations, giving your clients clear, organized evidence to support internal investigations, insurance claims or law enforcement enquiries.

Protect devices, wherever the work happens

Give your clients visibility into company-owned devices wherever they’re used. Set up automation policies to maintain compliance, whether their teams work remotely, in the office, or on the move.

Simplify secure employee offboarding

Keep track of devices assigned to departing employees, and help your clients manage the return process. If a device isn’t returned, your team can quickly start the recovery process.
