What You Can Do With Senturo

Know where your devices are, understand what’s happening to them, and take action when it matters. Senturo gives you the almost real-time device visibility to keep track of your fleet, and the tools to protect it.

From remote security and device recovery to broadcast messaging, Geofencing, and Reporting API access, Senturo helps you keep devices visible, accounted for and easier to recover throughout their whole lifecycle.

Missing Mode

Act Fast When a Device Goes Missing

Switch a device into an active recovery state for real-time location reporting and even more recovery information. Give your teams what they need to investigate faster, make better-informed decisions, and coordinate recovery efforts more effectively.

Explore Missing Mode
Senturo Lock

Secure the Device Without Slowing Recovery

Secure supported devices remotely without interrupting recovery. Display return instructions, keep receiving location and network updates, and restore access remotely once you know the device is safe.

Explore Senturo Lock
Remote Wipe

Protect Data Without Losing Track

Keep your data out of the wrong hands. Wipe your Windows and macOS device remotely and keep tracking it, so you can get it back faster.

Explore Remote Wipe
Broadcast Messaging

Messages Your Devices When it Matters

Send messages straight to your managed devices. Broadcast them instantly, schedule them for later, set up recurring messages, or automate security messages through Senturo Security Policy Automations.

Explore Broadcast Messaging
Geofencing

Turn Device Movement Into an Automatic Response

Set up approved or restricted (or both!) areas for your managed devices, and automatically trigger alerts, security actions or recovery steps when they enter or leave them.

Explore Geofencing
