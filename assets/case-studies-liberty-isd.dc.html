Liberty ISD Enhances Device Recovery and District Communication with Senturo

The IT department at Liberty ISD, led by Leslie Waller, Network Administrator, faced growing challenges with device loss, student login issues, and outdated fleet management processes. After implementing Senturo, Liberty ISD has significantly reduced lost devices, streamlined troubleshooting, and improved IT efficiency.

About the client

About Liberty ISD

Liberty Independent School District (ISD), located east of Houston, Texas, serves approximately 2,400 students and manages over 3,500 district-owned devices. The district is committed to providing high-quality education while leveraging modern technology to enhance student learning and ensure security. Liberty ISD values innovation, student safety, and financial accountability, making technology a critical part of its educational mission.

01 · The challenge

The Challenge

With thousands of Chromebooks, Windows laptops, and iPads deployed across multiple campuses, Liberty ISD’s IT team faced ongoing challenges:

  • Lost or misplaced devices disrupted student learning and led to unnecessary replacement costs.
  • Manual device tracking was time-consuming and diverted IT staff from more strategic initiatives.
  • Communication gaps made it difficult to send real-time updates to students and staff across multiple operating systems.
02 · The solution

The Solution:

Liberty ISD selected Senturo to streamline device management, enhance security, and improve district-wide communication. Senturo’s seamless integration with Google Admin allowed for effortless deployment, while its highly accurate location tracking enabled fast recovery of lost devices. During the pilot, Liberty ISD also discovered Senturo’s built-in one-way messaging system, an unexpected but highly effective tool that allowed them to send important updates to devices without relying on email or chat.

Always know the location of your fleet of devices, no matter how dispersed they are. Senturo empowers you to discover your devices’ exact real-time locations, and view them easily on Google Maps.

03 · The results

The Results

Faster Device Recovery & Reduced Losses

Senturo has significantly sped up device recovery efforts, reducing unnecessary spending on lost or misplaced hardware. This efficiency has allowed the IT team to focus on more important initiatives that support student learning.

Improved Communication & Loaner Device Management

The multi-OS messaging system has become an essential tool for sending district-wide updates and has helped resolve loaner device mix-ups quickly, ensuring students and staff receive the correct devices without delays.

Time Savings for IT Staff

By automating location tracking and security features, Senturo has freed up valuable time for Liberty ISD’s IT team, enabling them to shift their focus to strategic projects that support the district’s high expectations for students, faculty, and staff.

Key Features Used

  • Fleet Map for real-time device tracking

  • Automated Lock activation to secure misplaced devices

  • Historical geolocation reports for asset tracking

  • IP monitoring for network security

  • Multi-OS one-way messaging system for instant district-wide communication

04 · Partnership

A Lasting Partnership

Senturo has become a critical tool in Liberty ISD’s commitment to innovation, security, and financial accountability. By minimizing device loss, improving communication, and streamlining IT operations, Senturo helps the district remain good stewards of taxpayer dollars while supporting a safe and effective learning environment.

Why Liberty ISD Recommends Senturo

For other school districts looking to reduce device loss, simplify IT operations, and improve communication, Senturo is a must-have. Its ease of use, accurate tracking, and seamless messaging capabilities make it an essential tool for managing a large fleet of devices efficiently—helping districts build champions in academics, character, and community.
