Automate Faculty Policies

Set Up Geofencing & Alerts

Keep faculty computers where they should be: in a specific lab or faculty building, on campus, or anywhere else you decide. Simply set the territory - from meters to thousands of miles. You’ll get alerts when a device leaves its designated zone and when it returns. Need tighter security? Simply assign your pick of automated security actions - such as remote screen lock - which will deploy on alert.

Block Unauthorized Networks

With Senturo, faculty devices stay firmly on faculty-approved networks. Simply assign your choice of network rules and automated security actions. If a device tries to connect to unauthorized VPNs, personal smartphone hotspots, or insecure networks, you’ll be notified - and your action deployed - immediately.

One-Way Broadcasts to All Devices

Make organizing people and processes easier with one-way messaging. Send critical alerts for faculty closures, fire drill reminders, or natural hazard. Send gentle reminders for device loan returns, too. Customize your messages and schedule & send them whenever you like.
