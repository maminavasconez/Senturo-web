Field Services

Improve operational efficiency & reduce device loss

Whether you’re in construction, healthcare, plumbing, HVAC, engineering, utilities, real estate, agriculture, home services, or logistics: keeping track of your team members in the field while maintaining the sustainability of your mobile device fleet is critical.

Senturo ensures the safety & efficiency of your team through real-time multi-OS mobile device geo-tracking, time-stamped geofencing alerts when team members and devices enter & leave designated areas while at the same time greatly improving your mobile device security posture and loss/recovery rates.

Mobile IT Fleet Management

Unparalleled Fleet Visibility 24/7

Senturo empowers you to monitor the real-time locations of any device in your fleet and review its location history from anywhere in the world. Gain instant visibility of your entire fleet on Google Maps, or focus in on a specific device for detailed tracking.

Ensure Devices Stay in Designated Areas

Is a device designated to a specific site? Configure geofences to receive alerts if it leaves the assigned area, and automatically lock the device. Additionally, you can display a message on the device to instruct your employees—or a potential thief—to return it to the correct location.

Security Monitoring

Get a Snapshot of the Security Status of Your Entire Fleet

Access a snapshot of the security status for your entire fleet through your Senturo dashboard. Quickly view how many devices are outside their designated geofences, how many have connected to unauthorized VPN networks, how many are currently set to 'missing' mode, and more.

Receive Security Alerts Regarding Networks and Locations

Tackle before they escalate by conducting quick check-ins of your device inventory. Receive alerts if a device hasn’t checked in within the time limits you set. With the ability to track and locate all your devices, you can effectively eliminate ambiguity.

Customized Cybersecurity

Stay Compliant With International Data Security

Senturo is equipped with robust security and privacy protocols that you can tailor to align with your own policies. Importantly, it does not track your employees' personal data. As an added benefit, every action taken in Senturo is recorded, allowing you to prevent any potential abuse.

Resolve Incidents Fast

Save Time on Tracking Down Misplaced or Lost Devices

Quickly set a device to 'missing' mode to secure it and effortlessly trace its last known locations—no extensive research required. Discovering it's just misplaced? Easily locate and secure the device, deactivate 'missing' mode, and seamlessly reintegrate it into your operations.

Remotely Lock and Wipe Devices to Safeguard Sensitive Documents

Set a device to 'missing' mode to secure it and easily trace its last known locations. This mode not only locks the device but also allows for a remote wipe if it's confirmed to be lost, ensuring your data remains protected.
