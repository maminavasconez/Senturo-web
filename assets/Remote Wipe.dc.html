Overview

Protect Your Data

Sometimes the data matters more than the hardware. Protect it first, then keep working to get the device back.

That’s why Wipe lets you remove personal and sensitive data remotely. Tracking carries on, so you can still monitor the device, even after the data is gone.

Pair Wipe with Senturo Missing Mode, Senturo Lock, and the rest of your recovery actions to build a security response that’s right for whatever you’re handling.
