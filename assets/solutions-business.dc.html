Streamlined Management

Organize & Monitor Your Entire Fleet with Ease

Work from anywhere. Hire from everywhere. That’s the beauty of today’s mobile workforce. But without clear oversight of where devices—and team members—are operating, you risk security breaches and compliance violations if devices access data in the wrong jurisdictions as well as financial and operational headaches from lost or misplaced devices.

Mobile IT Fleet Management

Get Total Fleet Visibility 24/7

Senturo gives you complete visibility of your mobile devices in real time, wherever they are in the world. View your entire fleet on Google Maps, or drill down to individual devices. Need a location history? No problem. Get a clear audit trail in just a couple of taps.

Protect Sensitive Data with Geofencing

Want to make sure your devices stay in an authorized location, such as your business park, state, or insured territory? Simply set the area - ranging from 50 metres to thousands of miles - and it’s done. You’ll get an alert when a device wanders outside its allowed boundaries or enters a forbidden zone. Need even tighter security? Simply assign your pick of security actions - such as remote screen lock - which will automatically deploy on alert.

Security with Privacy

Support Your Remote Workforce

Senturo is cross-platform, and that means you can have the confidence to resource your remote workforce with the right device for the right job: Macs for designers, Windows for your sales team, whatever they need. It’s all good.

And, yes, Senturo will track and locate lost devices, but it’s not Big Brother. Because it monitors device location, not online activity, you can reassure your people that their privacy is respected. Senturo’s customizable, flexible tracking will double down on security when a device is at risk, but otherwise you can be light-touch and barely-there.

Resolve Incidents Fast

Remotely Lock Missing Devices

Not sure if a device has been stolen? All it takes is a couple of taps on your Senturo dashboard to set it to ‘missing’ and lock it remotely while you track and locate it. Not stolen, just misplaced? Simply locate and secure it, switch off missing mode and it’s back in business.

Remotely Wipe Compromised Devices

Double down on best-practice access control if a device falls into the wrong hands. Simply lock it down with Senturo’s instant remote wipe functionality, removing corporate data from the dangers of unauthorized access completely. You’ve the flexible option of taking screenshots to aid recoverability, too

Automate Company Policies

Enhance Compliance

SOC 2, PCI DSS, ISO 27001, or your own corporate compliance policy - whichever your data security compliance framework, Senturo is packed with automated controls that will help you meet the standards. Data integrity & confidentiality, threat response, and access control are all built in.

Ensure Usage of Corporate VPNs

Keep your devices firmly on approved networks. Simply assign your choice of network rules and automated security actions. Monitor the usage of your VPNs, and get automatic notifications when not complied.

One-Way Broadcasts to All Devices

Make life easier with one-way messaging. Send critical alerts for district outages, office closures, fire drill reminders or natural hazard instructions. Send gentle reminders for device check-ins, too. Customize your messages and schedule & send them whenever you like.

Stay in Control

Senturo’s access permission levels and detailed audit trails keeps all Senturo activity visible, accountable and compliant at all times. And with two-factor authentication, you can be confident that only authorized team members get access.
