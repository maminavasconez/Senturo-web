Missing Mode

Track Your Missing Devices in Real Time

When a device is reported lost, stolen or unreturned, switch to Missing Mode. Senturo ramps up location updates, captures recovery evidence and lets you take remote security actions — all in one place.

Setup steps

How to Use Missing Mode

1

Mark your device as missing

Update its status from the device record, fleet list or a connected platform such as Incident IQ.

2

Check the information coming in

Follow location updates, check network details, and see who last signed in. Review the screenshots on supported devices.

3

Choose the action that’s appropriate for your situation

Choose any or all of these: lock the device, protect the data, show on-screen return instructions, or send a message.

4

Finish the recovery

Once you’ve recovered the device, mark it as secured and hold on to the incident record for future reference.

Actions

Take Action to Recover Your Device

Lock your device

Make it easier to return

Display contact details and return instructions on the lock screen to help the finder get the device back to you.

Customize your message

Send clear instructions using text and images to tailor the message to the situation.

Protect your organization’s data

Device unrecoverable? Use remote wipe to remove its data.

Note: Your options for locking and wiping a device depend on its operating system and connected MDM.

Automation

Start Missing Mode Automatically

Set your security policies to activate Missing Mode when a device meets a condition you define. Your recovery process will kick off without waiting for you or your team to switch it on manually.

When a device leaves an approved area

Create a geofenced area to activate Missing Mode when a device leaves an approved area, or enters a non-approved place.

When a device stays offline

Automated Missing Mode will start when a device has not connected for a period you specify.

At a time you choose

Set a date and time to change device status, or request updated information.

Using your own conditions

Build policies around device location, network activity or security status.
